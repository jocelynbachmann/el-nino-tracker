seas5_monthly_grid.csv
ECMWF SEAS5 forecast from 2026-10-01, mean of 51 ensemble runs.
Area 46.5-51.5N, 133.5-120.5W, 1 deg grid. Months 2026-10 to 2027-03.

month                 YYYY-MM, monthly mean
lat, lon              cell centre (deg; lon negative = west)
is_ocean              False = land, ocean columns empty
sea_surface_height_m  sea height above geoid (m)
surface_temp_c        sea surface temp (C)
wind_speed_ms         10 m wind speed (m/s)
north_wind_ms         10 m wind, + = toward north (m/s)
east_wind_ms          10 m wind, + = toward east (m/s)
salinity_psu          surface salinity (PSU)
*_spread              std dev across the 51 runs (uncertainty)
*_source              model = real data; proxy/approx = estimated
